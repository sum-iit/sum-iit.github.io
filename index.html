I have recently submitted my Ph.D. thesis in Applied Mathematics at Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad, under the supervision of Dr. Bharath B.N., Associate Professor at IIT Dharwad. My Ph.D. research focused on the mathematical foundations of federated learning, online learning, generalization, and optimization, with particular emphasis on non-convex optimization landscapes, distributed learning settings, and rigorous performance guarantees for modern learning algorithms.

My academic journey began with an M.Sc. in Mathematics, where I specialized in Real Analysis, Functional Analysis, Operator Theory, Probability, and Statistical Learning Theory. This strong mathematical background has shaped my approach to research—grounded in rigorous proofs, while also mindful of computational challenges. Alongside theory, I also explore applied machine learning, especially in Python, and enjoy bridging abstract concepts with practical implementations.

I have recently joined Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), Abu Dhabi as a visiting research student, working with Prof. Mohsen Guizani. At MBZUAI, my current work is moving toward Trustworthy AI, including certified robustness for LLM agents, agentic AI systems, and related questions on reliability, safety, and robustness in modern AI. I am actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities where I can continue to deepen my research in theoretical machine learning and trustworthy AI while collaborating with interdisciplinary teams.
